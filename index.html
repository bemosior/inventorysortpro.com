How It Works

Photo by photo.
Decision by decision. Done.

No dashboard to configure. No learning curve to justify. Drop photos in, triage in sequence, export results.

1
Drop photos into the watch folder

Drag in a batch of item photos — JPEG, PNG, HEIC, whatever comes off the phone. The system ingests, converts, and queues them automatically.

No upload wizard. No renaming.
2
Triage one item at a time

Each photo fills the screen. You decide: Accept, Reject, or Special Case. One tap or keystroke. Move on. The decision is locked — no accidental undo, no second-guessing a batch you already processed.

Context note? Add it inline. It follows the item.
3
Review, batch, and export

Sorted items collect into clean result sets. Review flagged items as a group, export your decision log, or hand off to the next step in your workflow.

CSV export. Simple enough to email to a client.
